## Why claims fail

[Four colluding verifiers](#four-colluding-verifiers-many-verifiers). Claim: the party cannot prove, for any citizen, that it holds every one of that citizen's credential uses of some kind (every alcohol purchase, say). It fails: measured 2.

- [Agent named in the holder's records](https://bakobo.github.io/keri-priv-analysis-suite/attacks/#agent-named-in-the-holders-records-agent-end-role "Checking a holder identifier tells the verifier which agent serves it. One agent serves all of a citizen's identifiers, so naming the same agent ties them together.") is enough on its own. Cause: keripy bab95c16 src/keri/core/eventing.py:4778 retains each holder AID's agent end role; keria 928ce26a src/keria/app/agenting.py:362 serves the same citizen's AIDs from one agent. Owner: the KERIA hosted-agent operator and SignifyPy holder.
- [Neighbouring issuance anchors](https://bakobo.github.io/keri-priv-analysis-suite/attacks/#neighbouring-issuance-anchors-kel-anchor-adjacency "Each issuance is recorded in order in the issuer's public key event log. When one citizen's copies are issued one after another, their records sit next to each other, which ties them together.") is enough on its own. Cause: signifypy 2e9772cb signify/app/credentialing.py:759 anchors each issuance in the issuer's next interaction event, as issued. Owner: SignifyPy issuance and the KERIA issuer.
- [First-seen times in the issuer's log](https://bakobo.github.io/keri-priv-analysis-suite/attacks/#first-seen-times-in-the-issuers-log-kel-first-seen "The issuer's public log carries the time each entry was first seen, from a server clock, untouched by any coarsening of the registry times. One citizen's issuances cluster in time.") is enough on its own. Cause: keripy bab95c16 src/keri/core/eventing.py:3131 stamps each KEL event's first-seen time from the wall clock, and the KEL replay carries it. Owner: KERIA's keripy event store.
- [Shared registry](https://bakobo.github.io/keri-priv-analysis-suite/attacks/#shared-registry-shared-registry "Two presented credentials that point to the same registry, the public list that records whether each credential is still valid. An issuer that puts a citizen's credentials in one registry ties them together for anyone who reads it.") is enough on its own. Cause: signifypy 2e9772cb signify/app/credentialing.py:735 issues every credential into the issuer's one named registry (status=regk). Owner: SignifyPy issuance and the KERIA issuer's registry policy.
- [Timestamp clustering](https://bakobo.github.io/keri-priv-analysis-suite/attacks/#timestamp-clustering-timestamp-clustering "Every credential's registry entry carries the time it was issued, to the microsecond. One citizen's copies are issued milliseconds apart, so their times stand out as a cluster.") is enough on its own. Cause: signifypy 2e9772cb signify/app/credentialing.py:738 stamps the credential and its iss event with the client's clock, to the microsecond. Owner: SignifyPy issuance and the KERIA issuer.

## Measurement limits

- Verifier-side bits are a lower bound on what presentation bytes can reveal: the scorer counts declared attributes, while a selectively disclosed age-band presentation can carry a full birthDate (tick 6hat).

## Parties

### A hacker who breaks into every issuing home (all-homes-hacker)

Someone who breaks into State systems and takes everything those systems hold. This party holds [issuing home 0](https://bakobo.github.io/keri-priv-analysis-suite/glossary/#compartment "One of the State's servers that issue credentials: its database and its logs."), [issuing home 1](https://bakobo.github.io/keri-priv-analysis-suite/glossary/#compartment "One of the State's servers that issue credentials: its database and its logs."), [issuing home 2](https://bakobo.github.io/keri-priv-analysis-suite/glossary/#compartment "One of the State's servers that issue credentials: its database and its logs."), [issuing home 3](https://bakobo.github.io/keri-priv-analysis-suite/glossary/#compartment "One of the State's servers that issue credentials: its database and its logs."), [the public record](https://bakobo.github.io/keri-priv-analysis-suite/glossary/#compartment "Issuers' key event logs, credential registries' logs and published batches, which anyone can read.").

What the adapter put in each:

- issuing home 0: One KERIA agency hosting the issuer's agent: its stores and its log. A credential row's management is the holder's agent AID, read from the end-role record the holder's OOBI left; KERIA has no management AID, and one agent serves all of a citizen's AIDs.
- issuing home 1: One KERIA agency hosting the issuer's agent: its stores and its log. A credential row's management is the holder's agent AID, read from the end-role record the holder's OOBI left; KERIA has no management AID, and one agent serves all of a citizen's AIDs.
- issuing home 2: One KERIA agency hosting the issuer's agent: its stores and its log. A credential row's management is the holder's agent AID, read from the end-role record the holder's OOBI left; KERIA has no management AID, and one agent serves all of a citizen's AIDs.
- issuing home 3: One KERIA agency hosting the issuer's agent: its stores and its log. A credential row's management is the holder's agent AID, read from the end-role record the holder's OOBI left; KERIA has no management AID, and one agent serves all of a citizen's AIDs.
